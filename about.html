About iLearnDeen

Free beta

iLearnDeen is a set of short games that help children from Kindergarten to Year 9 learn about Islam according to the teachings of the Ahl al-Bayt (ʿa). Children can play at home, in class, or at community programs, where a teacher can show the questions on a big screen and play in teams.

The games cover six units: knowing Allah and our beliefs, worship in practice, the 14 Maʿṣūmīn, stories of the Prophets, the Qur'an and the Islamic year, and good character. Special packs are ready for occasions such as 15 Shaʿbān, 13 Rajab and Eid al-Ghadīr.

Our content rules

  • Only Shia (Imāmī) teachings.
  • Rulings (fiqh) only where all our marājiʿ agree. For anything else, please check your marjaʿ.
  • No pictures of the Prophets, the Imams, Sayyidah Fāṭimah (ʿa) or other holy figures.
  • Muḥarram and Karbalāʾ are introduced gently, focusing on values.
  • All wording is written fresh for this app.

Sources

Every answer shows its source. The main sources are:

  • The Holy Qur'an, with ʿAllāmah Ṭabāṭabāʾī's Tafsīr al-Mīzān
  • al-Kulaynī, al-Kāfī; Shaykh al-Ṣadūq, Kamāl al-Dīn, al-Iʿtiqādāt and ʿUyūn Akhbār al-Riḍā
  • Shaykh al-Mufīd, al-Irshād
  • Nahj al-Balāghah and al-Ṣaḥīfah al-Sajjādiyyah
  • Shaykh ʿAbbās al-Qummī, Mafātīḥ al-Jinān
  • The books of rulings (Tawḍīḥ al-Masāʾil) of the marājiʿ

The content is being reviewed by scholars. If you notice anything that needs correcting, please let us know.

Install on your phone

  • Android (Chrome): tap the ⋮ menu → Add to home screen (or Install app) → choose Install, not “Create shortcut”.
  • iPhone or iPad (Safari): tap Share (square with an up arrow) → Add to Home Screen → Add.

Once installed, iLearnDeen opens full-screen and works offline.
